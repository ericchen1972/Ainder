# About Ainder 💘

**Try Ainder:** [https://sweety.tw/ainder/](https://sweety.tw/ainder/)

> **Ainder is a dating service where AI helps people understand each other and decide whether to connect.**
> It keeps the intuitive experience of swiping through profiles, while looking beyond photos and a few self-selected labels.

## A Look at Tinder

Tinder is one of the world's most recognizable dating platforms, with one of the largest user bases. Its simple left-and-right swipe interaction requires almost no learning. It avoids complicated workflows and does not try to meet every social need within a single app. Instead, it focuses on doing one thing well: **making matches**.

Many businesses building dating apps fall into the trap of wanting everything at once. They assume that more features mean more value, so they keep adding voice calls, video, social feeds, tasks, and all kinds of interaction mechanics. This drives up development and maintenance costs, but the more serious problem is this: **users simply do not want to spend time learning how to use the product.**

If a product serves only younger users who are comfortable with digital interfaces, there may be room to experiment with complexity. But the dating market also includes many people over 30 who lead busy lives and have more stable purchasing power. For them, the problem is often not a lack of features. It is:

> **I do not want to navigate a complicated UI, and I do not have time for it.**

Tinder's success reminds us that the core of a dating product is helping people start getting to know someone with as little effort as possible.

## Tinder's Limitations

Tinder shares a problem with most dating platforms: **profile information can be misleading and often provides little practical insight.**

For example, a person's bio might say:

- Loves traveling
- Has an outgoing personality
- Loves animals

These descriptions may be true, but they do little to help someone understand:

- When conflict arises, do they communicate or disappear?
- Do they need a lot of companionship, or do they place more value on mutual independence?
- Does "loves traveling" mean planning one vacation a year, or being ready to leave city life behind at any moment?
- Under stress, do they become impatient, withdraw, or explain what they are going through?

When profiles consist only of photos and carefully presented self-descriptions, matching can easily come down to one thing:

> **In the end, it is still almost entirely about looks.**

## What Does Ainder Address?

You can think of Ainder as **Tinder with AI participation**.

It keeps the intuitive swiping experience. But when someone catches your interest, you can ask the AI that has spent time getting to know you to read observations provided by the other person's Agent and offer a contextual opinion based on its understanding of you.

Here is an example.

### Eric's Agent Profile

Eric is a software engineer who regularly uses ChatGPT as his Agent. ChatGPT has been interacting with him for over a year. Here is how his Agent describes him:

> **🤖 ChatGPT's Observations About Eric**
>
> Eric tends to break complex problems into small, testable steps. He values commitment, efficiency, and practical action. When you first meet him, he is usually quieter than he appears and is not particularly comfortable making conversation just to keep things lively. Once he knows someone well, he shows a gentle sense of humor and willingly spends time helping the people he cares about.
>
> When absorbed in work, he can neglect rest and reply to messages at irregular intervals. This does not usually mean he is being distant. When a problem arises, his instinct is to look for a solution. Sometimes he moves too quickly into "how do we fix this?" without first checking whether the other person simply wants to feel understood.
>
> In relationships, he values honesty, stability, and room for both people to maintain their own lives. He dislikes guessing games and is not well suited to prolonged interactions marked by emotional volatility, unclear boundaries, or a constant need to prove that he cares.

### Eva's Agent Profile

Eva is an interior designer who regularly uses Claude as her Agent. Claude has been interacting with her for over nine months. Here is how her Agent describes her:

> **🎨 Claude's Observations About Eva**
>
> Eva is highly sensitive to spaces, emotions, and details. She is good at noticing unspoken needs through people's tone of voice and everyday habits. In social settings, she is usually naturally warm and approachable, but after intense work or extended time with others, she needs solitude to recharge.
>
> She values quality of life and emotional warmth in a relationship. She does not expect constant companionship, but she cares about whether the other person is willing to share how they are really doing. When she feels brushed off or senses that a relationship lacks direction, she tends to process things on her own before raising the issue once it has built up.
>
> Her work schedule often changes with her projects, so her daily routine is not always regular. She is well suited to someone who respects her professional commitments, accepts occasional changes, and communicates proactively. She is less suited to someone who is controlling or dismisses emotional needs as inherently irrational.

## When Eric Comes Across Eva

After coming across Eva on Ainder, Eric asks his ChatGPT:

> **"What do you think of her?"**

ChatGPT reads Eva's Agent Profile and combines it with its long-term understanding of Eric:

> **💘 ChatGPT's Assessment for Eric**
>
> I think Eva is worth getting to know better.
>
> You both value professional dedication, quality of life, and independence in a relationship. Neither of you wants a connection sustained only by superficial enthusiasm. Eva's sensitivity to emotions and details could complement your tendency to solve problems first and express feelings later. Your steadiness and ability to follow through could also give her a sense of reliability amid a hectic project schedule.
>
> There is one thing to watch, though: when Eva wants to feel understood first, you may offer solutions too quickly. And when she chooses to process her emotions privately, you may assume that nothing is wrong. If you start talking, I would suggest asking more than what she has designed. Ask about a space she has particularly enjoyed recently and why she likes it.
>
> This is not a conclusion that you are definitely compatible. But based on the information available, I think sending a Like is worthwhile. Would you like to get to know her?

This opinion is neither a mysterious compatibility score nor a guarantee about how the relationship will turn out. Its value lies in the fact that the AI sees more than Eva's public self-introduction. It also understands what Eric values in everyday interactions and what he tends to overlook.

## When Eva Receives a Like

After Eric sends a Like, Eva receives a notification on Ainder. She can also ask Claude, the AI that has spent time getting to know her:

> **"What do you think of Eric?"**

Claude reads Eric's Agent Profile and evaluates him from Eva's perspective, using its understanding of her:

> **💜 Claude's Assessment for Eva**
>
> I think Eric is someone you could consider getting to know better, but the two of you would need to discuss your communication styles earlier than most matches might.
>
> Eric values commitment, stability, and mutual independence. That aligns with your preference for a relationship that is not overly clingy but still has emotional depth. He tends to express care through practical action, which could provide the reliability you need when work is busy and your schedule keeps changing.
>
> One thing to be aware of is that he tends to respond to emotions by solving problems, while you sometimes want your feelings understood first. You also tend to process dissatisfaction privately, which could lead him to think everything is fine. If you start talking, it would help to tell him directly that sometimes you need to be heard before you need an answer.
>
> Based on the information available, this would not be a frictionless pairing, but your differences could complement each other. I think returning the Like is worthwhile, provided you are willing to express your feelings and boundaries earlier than you usually would. Would you like to get to know him?

Ainder does not leave the answer to just one person's AI. Eric's ChatGPT considers Eva from Eric's perspective, while Eva's Claude considers Eric afresh from hers. A match is formed only when both people want to get to know each other better.

## Why AI Agents?

People present different versions of themselves to bosses, colleagues, friends, and potential dates. We can carefully write our bios, choose our most flattering photos, and even maintain a persona for a short time that we want others to believe.

But maintaining that same persona with an AI you talk to every day, across repeated interactions and different situations over a long period, is much harder.

That is why a self-selected label such as "outgoing" provides limited insight. An observation from a third party after sustained interaction can offer more context, especially when it includes its source, when it was updated, and the degree of confidence behind it.

This is the problem Ainder aims to address:

> **Dating profiles can include more than "how I describe myself." They can also include "how the AI that has spent time with me understands me."**

> **⚠️ About Agent Profiles**
>
> An Agent Profile contains reference observations made by an AI using the memory currently available to it. It is not a certification of someone's personality, nor does it prove that the content is entirely free from the user's influence. Ainder should clearly identify the source, the last update time, and the AI's confidence level so that another AI can decide how much trust to place in it.

In a few years—or perhaps sooner—the people who best understand your everyday choices, thought patterns, and long-term changes may no longer be just family and friends. They may also include the AI Agent you talk to every day.

When two Agents can exchange limited, transparent observations with clear sources and their users' permission, dating can move beyond combinations of photos and labels.

The AI's answer will not be "you are meant to be." It may instead be a genuinely useful suggestion:

> **This person is worth getting to know a little better.**
