# Sweety Product Reference Image Design

## Goal

Create product imagery that can be passed to an AI as reliable visual reference material. The clothing must remain identifiable across every angle, while the display figure stays visually neutral and never becomes the focus.

## Deliverables

- Rename the two storefront categories to `MEN` and `WOMEN`.
- Create six product sets: three menswear sets and three womenswear sets.
- Each set contains one SlimWeb product main image and three product-description images.
- Total output: 24 images.
- Store final images under `/Volumes/1TB/Codex-Media/images/sweety-products/`.

## Image Formats

- Product main image: square 1:1 composition for SlimWeb product cards and product galleries.
- Product-description images: vertical 9:16, full-body composition.
- Every image has sufficient margin around the mannequin so feet, hems, sleeves, and garment edges are not cropped.

## Visual Direction

- Realistic clean exhibition-catalog photography.
- Use matte plastic retail display mannequins with naturally proportioned bodies.
- Heads are completely featureless: no eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, nostrils, lips, teeth, or painted facial marks.
- Neutral warm-white or light-gray seamless studio background.
- Soft, even studio lighting with restrained contact shadows.
- No text, logo, watermark, decorative furniture, handheld props, or elaborate scenery.
- Garments must remain the visual focus, with clear fabric texture, seams, silhouette, length, and layering.

## Product Sets

### MEN

1. Charcoal tailored suit, ivory shirt, restrained dark accessories, clean leather shoes.
2. Black leather jacket, simple neutral inner layer, dark indigo straight jeans, understated footwear.
3. Oatmeal casual jacket or overshirt, soft neutral knit or T-shirt, khaki relaxed trousers, minimal sneakers.

### WOMEN

1. Dusty-rose midi dress with a clean, wearable silhouette and understated shoes.
2. Taupe suit-inspired blazer and coordinated wide-leg trousers with a light neutral inner layer.
3. Cream short jacket, simple neutral top, straight blue jeans, understated shoes.

## Angle Set And Consistency

Each product receives four images:

1. Square main image: full-body front three-quarter pose.
2. Vertical image: straight front view.
3. Vertical image: side or rear three-quarter view.
4. Vertical image: back view with a slight natural turn.

For all four images in a set, preserve the exact same mannequin body, garment colors, fabric, seams, proportions, closures, hems, shoes, and accessories. Arms may move slightly to reveal the garment, but must not cover lapels, waistlines, pockets, or other important construction details.

## Acceptance Criteria

- 24 separate final image files are present.
- Six sets are visually distinct and match the requested clothing categories.
- Every set is internally consistent across all four views.
- Product main images are 1:1 and product-description images are 9:16.
- All mannequins are realistic matte plastic display figures with completely blank faces.
- No garment is cropped, obscured, branded, or altered between angles.
- Category names read `MEN` and `WOMEN` after MCP verification.
